## Background: two weights per exercise, not three

> *"there's no need for three different weights in one exercise. We are starting
> at a heavy but established weight and then moving to a higher weight."*

The current position-aware ramp produces three distinct weights
(`[T-2i, T-i, T, T, T]`). The owner wants **two**: an established weight, then
the target.

This is already validated by his own log. Sep 8 push ran two-weight patterns
throughout — DB Bench `35,35,40,40,40` at RPE 6,6,7,7,7; Pec Fly
`120,135,135,135` at 6,7,8,8 — and the session came in at **43 minutes**, inside
the 45-minute lifting budget. The three-weight Sep 9 legs session ran longer.

The reframe that makes this work: **the sets at the established weight are the
warm-up.** They are productive volume at a real load, not junk sets at a token
weight.

## PHASE 2 — reorder the triceps pair

> *"cant do 20s after pushdowns"* — Skull Crusher, Sep 8

Skull Crusher has dropped every time it follows Rope Pushdown — Aug 27
(`20×10,8,5` then down to 12), Sep 5 (`20×8,6` then down to 15), Sep 8
(`15×10×4`, chip `down`). Same muscle group, and the pushdowns pre-exhaust it.

**Swap the default order: Skull Crusher before Rope Pushdown.** Skull Crusher is
the load-sensitive one, so it gets the fresher slot. Reflected in the Phase 1
table above.

## PHASE 3 — 45-minute cardio reminder

> *"Instead of automatically switching to cardio, add a reminder at 45 mins that
> asks me to switch to cardio."*

The owner lifted through the cardio block on Sep 9, so `liftingMin` recorded 52
when actual lifting was ~39 — the stairmaster time was counted as lifting.

- At **45 minutes of lifting time**, show a non-blocking prompt asking whether to
  switch to cardio.
- **Do not switch automatically.** The prompt offers switch / dismiss.
- If dismissed, do not nag repeatedly — at most one further reminder, well later.
- Switching starts the cardio timer and stops accruing lifting time.
- The reminder must survive app backgrounding, consistent with the existing timer
  persistence.

## PHASE 4 — Calf Raise derivation bug

Calf Raise derived **35** and suggested `down` on Sep 9, despite:

```
Aug 25   45×15×3 @ RPE 6
Sep  3   45×15×4 @ RPE 6,6,7,7
```

Both sessions hit target reps comfortably within range. Goblet Squat — same
superset, same sessions — derived correctly at 50, so this is not the superset
exclusion logic generally.

**Suspected cause:** Calf Raise's rep target is a range (15–20). If the engine
scores against the upper bound, 15 reads as "missed" and triggers `down`.

Check how rep-range bounds are configured for high-rep movements and ensure
success is scored against the **lower** bound, per the Aug 19 rep-range rule.

Add a fixture asserting Calf Raise derives **45** and suggests `hold` or `up`
from that history.
